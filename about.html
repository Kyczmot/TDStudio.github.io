About TD Studio

TD Studio is a small game development studio focused on delivering accessible, polished and enjoyable games. We care about fair design, clear communication and respecting our players' time.

What We Care About

Our games are designed to be relaxing, readable and fair. We avoid predatory monetisation, make mechanics as clear as possible and aim for experiences you can pick up for a short session or enjoy for longer playthroughs.

  • Fair design: no pay-to-win, no confusing currencies.
  • Clarity: clean UI and mechanics you can understand at a glance.
  • Respect for time: you decide how long to play – no aggressive timers.

Contact & Support

If you have questions, feedback or need support for any of our games, you can reach us at support@tdstudio.uk.
